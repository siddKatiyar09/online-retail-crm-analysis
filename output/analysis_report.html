Executive Summary

  • After CRM-focused cleaning, the dataset retains 397,884 valid transaction rows and 4,338 identified customers.
  • 24.9% of raw rows have no CustomerID, so anonymous orders should be treated separately from lifecycle CRM programs.
  • United Kingdom contributes $7,308,392 and dominates geographic concentration.
  • The revenue peak lands in 2011-11 at $1,161,817, supporting a strong Q4 demand pattern.
  • The highest-value RFM segment is Champions and the top ML cluster is Champions.

Data Quality and Scope

  • 541,909 raw transactions from 2010-12-01 08:26:00 to 2011-12-09 12:50:00.
  • 135,080 rows (24.9%) have no CustomerID.
  • 9,288 rows (1.7%) are cancellations.
  • 10,624 rows (2.0%) have negative quantities.
  • 2,517 rows (0.5%) have zero or negative pricing.

Recommended CRM Actions

  • Protect Champions with early-access offers, premium service, and referral incentives.
  • Move Core Growth customers toward loyalty by increasing repeat-order cadence and basket size.
  • Target Big-Ticket Occasionals with event-based replenishment reminders and curated bundles.
  • Launch win-back and onboarding automations for Hibernating and Promising customers.
